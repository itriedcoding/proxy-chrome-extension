# Privacy Policy

- **Data Collection**: We do not collect or store any personal information. 
- **Network Usage**: The extension fetches proxy lists directly from `geonode.com`. 
- **Credentials**: If users use authorized proxies, credentials are not stored on any remote server; they exist only in your browser's memory during the session.
- **Proxy Logs**: We do not log the traffic passing through the proxies you select. Be aware that the proxy provider itself may have its own logging policy.
